NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · Planet X and SPHEREx

Watch any patch of the infrared sky change over time

Phantom Worlds plays NASA SPHEREx images of a position as a time series, in six detectors and 102 bands, so anyone can see what moves or changes. Then it reads what a source is probably made of, and says how sure it is.

What you can do

ViewerPlay any position as a time series. Subtract one visit from another, stack them, or colour them by date. Source detailsThe spectrum, the molecule readings and the most likely type, with the reasons behind the estimate. Search by lightFind sources by their molecule features and by how they change between visits. Signals and casesMachine checks, a dated prediction that new data settles, and review by people who answer before seeing each other. ProofEvery claim measured against a pass mark fixed before the results came in.
